\section{Station Metadata Dataset (GBFS)}
Our fourth source of data is current station information and live availability from the Lyft General Bikeshare Feed Specification (GBFS) feed\footnote{\url{https://gbfs.lyft.com/gbfs/2.3/bkn/en/gbfs.json}} - the same feed used by the backend.
Two endpoints are merged: \texttt{station\_information} (static: name, location, capacity) and \texttt{station\_status} (live: bikes/docks available, operational flags).
